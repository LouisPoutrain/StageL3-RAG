Fecal samples were collected as they are known to contain sufficient DNA for genetic analysis and their collection does not require animal handling or capture, thus minimizing disturbance and stress to the animals. To minimize potential contamination, each sample was collected using clean forceps and gloves changed between each collection site. Additionally, care was taken to ensure that sampling did not disturb the animals' habitat or routines.
